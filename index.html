About

I'm an incoming Mechanical Engineering freshman at Penn State who likes building things that move — 3D-printed mechanisms, welded sheet metal, and small electronics brought together into working hardware. Outside of class, most of my learning happens in the garage: taking things apart, modeling them in CAD, and rebuilding them better. The projects below are a mix of independent builds — more are on the way as I get further into my degree.

Drone-Controlled Pick-and-Place Robot
